The idea

A huge music library can still feel personal.

Spotify has millions of songs. The challenge is helping each listener find the few that feel just right.

751Mlisteners to serve

1personal listening experience

A shared Top 50 is a good start, but music taste is personal. One listener may replay mellow songs after work. Another may skip straight to high energy tracks.

Recommendations can learn from those everyday choices. A replay, a skip, or a song played to the end gives a clue about what someone likes.

For MIS 432, I built a small demo that uses listening patterns to suggest songs. It helped me see how the same idea can help any business make a customer experience feel more personal.

What I learned

Eight ideas that make the experience feel personal.

Here is the plain language version of what happens behind a recommendation.

👆
Actions tell a story
A replay, skip, or full listen can reveal more than a rating form.
🎧
Small clues add up
Patterns like replaying a style of music help describe a listener’s taste.
🤝
Similar listeners help each other
When people enjoy some of the same songs, a favorite from one may be a good discovery for the other.
🌙
Listening has a mood
Long, relaxed sessions may point to different music than quick skips on a busy morning.
🎼
Songs have neighbors too
Music with similar qualities can be grouped together, making it easier to suggest something new.
🔄
Taste keeps changing
A good recommendation experience has to keep up with new artists, habits, and moments.
🛡️
Someone has to watch the quality
Teams need to notice when recommendations stop feeling useful and improve them.
💚
Trust takes time to build
Years of listening history are hard for a competitor to recreate. That connection is part of the value.
A favorite example

One listening history. Many ways to use it.

Spotify Wrapped turns the year’s listening into a story people want to share.

The same listening history that helps shape recommendations can also create a year in review. Wrapped shows how one useful system can create more than one kind of value.

For this project, I added a small Wrapped style recap to the demo. It uses the same sample listening activity as the recommendations.

Three things I will take with me

The business lessons.

01
Use the signals you already have.
Clicks, purchases, and support questions can reveal what customers need. A business can learn a lot by paying attention to them.
02
Notice when the experience gets worse.
A system can keep running while becoming less helpful. Teams need to listen, check results, and respond.
03
Make personalization earn trust.
Useful recommendations can bring people back. They should feel relevant, respectful, and easy to control.
